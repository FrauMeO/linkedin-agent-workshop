## Post
The cheapest prototype quote can become an expensive production handoff.

Procurement should qualify the path to volume before placing the prototype order.

A supplier that supports both stages can retain the context built during quoting, DFM reviews, revisions, validation, tooling and quality planning. That continuity may reduce repeated onboarding, requalification and interpretation risk when production begins.

But continuity is valuable only if the supplier is ready for the intended process and volume.

Before awarding prototype work, confirm:

- Production capacity and quality systems
- Pricing at forecast volumes
- Tooling ownership and change control
- Technical support and responsiveness
- Contingency plans for critical components

This is not an argument to remain with one vendor at any cost. A strong prototype supplier may still lack the controls, equipment or resilience required for sustained production.

The better approach is to choose a partner with a controlled path from prototype to production—while keeping backup-source decisions risk-based.
